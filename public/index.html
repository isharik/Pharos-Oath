POLICY EVALUATION

OATH Gate checks:

  • Recipient and spender format
  • Pharos chain ID and connected wallet
  • Blocklist and optional trusted address list
  • Native transfer and ERC-20 approval caps
  • eth_call simulation and gas estimation before signing
Policies are browser-local. Clearing site data resets them. They cannot constrain transactions signed outside OATH. Blanket NFT operator approvals are not supported by this approval flow.
